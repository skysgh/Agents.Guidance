# Regulatory and Obligation Checklist


Use this checklist with [Regulatory and Obligation Domains](../catalogues/regulatory-obligations.md) and [Legal and Regulatory Context](../../../foundations/legal-context.md). It helps a team record the actual sources, duties, capabilities and evidence for a service. It is not legal advice and it is not a universal compliance declaration.

Use the prompts that match the service's consequences. Record the answer, owner, evidence or deliberate non-applicability rather than treating an unchecked box as proof of compliance.

## Scope and authority

- [ ] Have we recorded where the service is offered, accessed, consumed or made available?
- [ ] Have we recorded where it is operated, hosted, supported, processed and backed up?
- [ ] Have we identified the people, organisations, providers, data locations and downstream systems that widen the jurisdiction map?
- [ ] Have we identified the sector, public-service, contractual, accreditation and organisational contexts that may create duties?
- [ ] For each source, have we recorded its authority, territorial scope, affected subject, effective period and responsible adviser or owner?
- [ ] Have we checked whether a source is law, regulation, regulator direction, contract, policy, standard or a voluntary reference?

## People, purpose and information

- [ ] Can we state why the service exists and what outcome or decision it supports?
- [ ] Have we identified the people and organisations affected, including people who never use the interface?
- [ ] Have we identified what information is collected, inferred, created, changed, shared, exported, archived and deleted?
- [ ] Is each collection and use connected to an authorised purpose, lawful basis, consent or other recorded authority where required?
- [ ] Are minimisation, accuracy, correction, transparency and rights-handling needs visible?
- [ ] Have we identified automated decisions, ranking, profiling, eligibility, refusal or other high-impact behaviour that needs explanation, review or human involvement?

## Tracking and communication

- [ ] Have we identified cookies, browser storage, analytics, advertising tags, device identifiers, fingerprinting, location use and other observation?
- [ ] Is each tracking purpose, provider, data shared, expiry, authority or consent state and withdrawal behaviour recorded?
- [ ] Does withdrawal or refusal behave as promised without leaving hidden copies or continuing unnecessary tracking?
- [ ] Can people distinguish official service identity, important notices, payment requests and genuine communication from fraud or misleading messages?
- [ ] Are terms, charges, eligibility, consequences, status and error messages understandable for the people who must act on them?

## Protection, access and safety

- [ ] Is the protected resource, actor, context and authority for each important access decision identified?
- [ ] Are authentication, authorisation, classification, least privilege, secure defaults and separation of duties assigned to responsible boundaries?
- [ ] Are secrets, credentials, keys, logs, notifications, URLs, exports, caches, indexes, backups and provider copies included in the protection design?
- [ ] Have we considered abuse, fraud, vulnerable people, dangerous states, high-impact decisions and foreseeable harm?
- [ ] Are rate limits, warnings, approvals, human review, emergency behaviour, incident reporting and recovery needed?
- [ ] Can people with the supported abilities, devices, languages, connections and assistive technologies complete the relevant outcome?
- [ ] Is usability tested with representative people and tasks, including error recovery and content understanding?

## Records, retention and evidence

- [ ] Which decisions, transactions, communications, changes, approvals, refusals and incidents must remain trustworthy records?
- [ ] Which information must be retained, for what purpose and period, and who can authorise disposal?
- [ ] Have we identified legal holds, archival, public-records, financial, tax, audit, investigation and continuity requirements?
- [ ] Does the design distinguish authoritative records from caches, indexes, messages, reports, exports, warehouses, archives and backups?
- [ ] Can the organisation retrieve the right record, understand its provenance and show who did what, when, with which authority and information?
- [ ] Are correction, restriction, deletion, irreversible anonymisation, de-identification and disposal paths explicit?
- [ ] What evidence proves that records, retention, deletion and recovery behave correctly during failure and change?

## Money, content and suppliers

- [ ] Are payments, charges, refunds, benefits, payroll, tax information, reconciliation, disputes and financial records in scope?
- [ ] Does the organisation have the right to collect, store, transform, publish, share and retain the content, data, software and media used by the service?
- [ ] Are licences, attribution, confidentiality, publication restrictions, correction and takedown needs recorded?
- [ ] Are cross-border transfers, data residency, government access, supplier access and sub-processors understood?
- [ ] Do supplier contracts carry down security, privacy, records, availability, incident, deletion, portability and exit duties?
- [ ] Can the organisation recover, replace or exit a provider without losing required information, evidence or service continuity?

## Ownership and evidence

- [ ] Does every obligation have a responsible organisation, role, boundary or supplier?
- [ ] Is each duty translated into a people, process, technical or infrastructure capability?
- [ ] Is the evidence defined before implementation, including tests, records, reviews, monitoring, training, exercises and approvals?
- [ ] Are failure, incident, recovery, complaint, correction, appeal and escalation paths covered?
- [ ] Are gaps, assumptions, conflicts and deliberate non-applicability recorded with their consequences?
- [ ] Is there a review trigger when the audience, jurisdiction, data, supplier, hosting location, operating model or regulation changes?

## Handoff

- [ ] Has the architect mapped the sources, duties, boundaries, dependencies and evidence across the wider system?
- [ ] Has the technical lead checked that the required capabilities and evidence are achievable with the available team, technology and delivery path?
- [ ] Have developers received enough context to preserve the stakeholder promise and organisational obligation while implementing the local capability?
- [ ] Have testers, operations, security, privacy, records, accessibility, finance, assurance and other relevant specialists reviewed the parts within their authority?
- [ ] Has the service recorded what is designed, what is built, what is deferred and what evidence is still missing?
