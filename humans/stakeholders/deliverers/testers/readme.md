## The tester's responsibility

A tester checks whether the claimed behaviour and quality conditions hold under stated conditions. Testers challenge successful, invalid, denied, duplicated, concurrent, degraded and recovered paths where those conditions matter. They preserve the distinction between functional meaning, technical implementation and operational evidence.

A tester is not automatically the Product Owner, Business Analyst, architect, Support role, operator or maintainer. One person may hold several responsibilities, but testing evidence must remain distinct from support diagnosis, operational readiness and maintenance change authority.
