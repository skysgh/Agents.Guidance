# Human Guidance Role and Responsibility Coverage Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix is the Phase 1 output of the [Human Guidance Completeness Audit](HUMAN-GUIDANCE-AUDIT-PLAN.md). It classifies material responsibilities by the treatment they currently have or need. A role title is a candidate for review, not proof that a separate route is required.

The matrix asks whether a reader can discover the knowledge, authority, handoff, evidence and lifecycle consequence associated with a responsibility. ## Decisions for the next audit increment

The matrix establishes these working decisions:

1. Product Manager now has a dedicated route because the responsibility is material to strategic value, constraints and the Product Owner relationship.
2. Technical Analyst is a deliberate non-route. The responsibility remains visible in shared requirements and the stakeholder catalogue, with only minor discoverability refinement left for later.
3. Security, Privacy, Records, Monitoring, Change Control and Assurance should first receive a shared control-responsibility treatment. Dedicated routes should follow only where their authority, evidence and reader journey cannot be explained adequately in shared guidance.
4. Sponsor now has a dedicated route because the commitment, variance, escalation and whole-life accountability journey is distinct from Product Manager and Product Owner stewardship. Business owner remains without a dedicated route pending a later authority and handoff audit.
5. Support, Operations, Maintainer and specialist developer depth should be judged through lifecycle and cold-start evidence, not by matching the number of pages in the core routes.

## Matrix limits

This matrix is a coverage hypothesis, not proof that every route works. It does not yet trace one capability through every lifecycle stage, validate all authority claims with project evidence or establish that every control has sufficient operational and assurance evidence. Those checks belong to the next audit stages.
