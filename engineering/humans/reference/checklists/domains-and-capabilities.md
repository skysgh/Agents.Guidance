# Domains and Capabilities Checklist

Use this checklist with [Domains and Capabilities](../catalogues/domains-and-capabilities.md).

- [ ] The capability has a recognisable purpose and a responsible domain.
- [ ] The conceptual language used by people and connected systems is recorded before it is refined.
- [ ] The logical concepts, identities, relationships, states and rules are explicit.
- [ ] Similar words with different meanings have not been silently merged.
- [ ] The capability’s contract, lifecycle and effects are clear.
- [ ] Shared rules are implemented once at the responsible boundary rather than copied into sites or flows.
- [ ] Separation of concerns, high cohesion and low coupling are visible in the proposed structure.
- [ ] A service, repository, broker or other code type has been selected because its responsibility is real, not because its name is familiar.
- [ ] The capability can be used by the required sites and flows without exposing internal models.
